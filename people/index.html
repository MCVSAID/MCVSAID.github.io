Performers the director recognises, with their consent: stars get priority on any camera, and a camera can follow a performer. Nobody else is ever identified.

Add a performer

Take a picture from a camera while the performer is in view, in what they will perform in, then click them in the picture (or drag a box around them). Then add more of them (Add as: the performer), at least three: from each camera that will see them, from the front and the side, and one close enough that their face is clear.
