Users

Everyone who can log in, by name, each with their own password and one role. What a role may do is the same for everyone who has it; what each person does is recorded under their name.

The roles

Administrator: everything, and only at this PC. Operator: runs a show (cameras, streams, recording, shots, the director), at this PC or from a tablet on the camera network; cannot update, restart or pair the phones, delete recordings from them, or change passwords and accounts. Stage Shots: chooses shots on the Stage Shots page on a phone, and nothing else.

A new role holds at once for whoever is logged in to that account: given less, they are logged down, or out where the new role does not open the page they are on. A new password, or removing the account, logs it out everywhere.
