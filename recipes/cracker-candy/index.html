Cracker Candy

Salted crackers under a layer of butter toffee and chocolate, broken into pieces once it has set.

Ingredients

  • 45 salted saltine crackers (about 1½ sleeves)
  • 1 cup salted butter (2 sticks)
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Set up the tray: Heat the oven to 400°F (205°C). Line a cookie sheet with parchment and let it come up the sides of the pan. Lay out 45 salted saltine crackers (roughly 1½ sleeves) in one layer with the salty side facing up. It's okay if there are a few gaps.
  2. Cook the toffee: Put 1 cup salted butter (2 sticks) and 1 cup packed brown sugar in a small saucepan over medium heat. Once it reaches a vigorous boil, keep it boiling for 3 minutes. Stir only if it looks like it might scorch.
  3. Coat the crackers: Take the pan off the heat and pour the toffee across the crackers. Use a heatproof spatula to spread it out, and nudge back any crackers that drift out of place.
  4. Bake: Bake for roughly 5 minutes, until the toffee is bubbling.
  5. Add the chocolate: Switch the oven off. Scatter 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips over the hot toffee and put the tray back in the oven for 2 minutes. Then spread the softened chocolate into an even layer.
  6. Chill and break: Put it in the fridge for at least 30 minutes so the chocolate firms up. Break into pieces and enjoy.
