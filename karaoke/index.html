Teleprompter controls
Next singer puts their song on the singer’s screen from the top. The words are followed as they are sung; ▼ on the screen, or a page turner, moves on a line.

The line

    Signing up from phones

    Guests scan this to sign up, or open and enter the code .

    New night clears the line and makes a new code, so last night’s QR codes stop working. A line is also cleared by itself after 18 hours. Only first names and songs are kept.

    Songs

    Each song is a teleprompter script: write or paste its words on the Teleprompter page, named “Title – Artist”, a ## Verse or ## Chorus line before each part. The music is played from your own karaoke setup; type in only words you have the right to show.
